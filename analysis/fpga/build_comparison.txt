====================================================================
INTERPRETATION OF THE DIFF                          [HIGH unless noted]
====================================================================
The three PWM builds (16.53 / 10.81 / 9.53) and the two non-PWM builds
(13.39 Normal / 6.69 LS0allDA) differ in exactly one big structural way
in the output stage:

  iol_IREG_OREG:  10  10  10  |  96  96

In 13.39 and 6.69 all 96 RGB data pads use the IO output REGISTER
(IOLOGIC MODE=IREG_OREG, DATAMUX_OREG=IOLDO).  In the PWM builds only 10
of them do; the other 86 are driven combinationally out of the fabric.

Properties of the 96 IO registers in 13.39 (one-hop resolution, so HIGH):
  CLK : ALL 96 are clocked by global net 0, which in 13.39 is
        G_ULPCLK0/G_URPCLK0/G_LLPCLK0/G_LRPCLK0 <- G_HPFE0300 <- LDCC3
        <- G_JLLCPLL0CLKOP, i.e. PLL CLKOP in every quadrant.
  LSR : tied on all 96 -- the RGB output registers have NO reset.
  CE  : tied (always enabled) on 72 of 96;
        11 gated by Q2@31,32, 6 by Q4@31,32, the rest individually.

=> the "Normal" firmware clocks the RGB bus straight out of an IO register
   at the full CLKOP rate; the PWM firmware does not use the IO register at
   all.  That IS an output-stage protocol difference between the family
   that leaves our panel dead (13.39) and the family that responds (PWM).
   [HIGH that the difference exists and what it is;
    NOT RESOLVED what the panel-visible consequence is.]

Secondary differences:
  * distributed LUT-RAM is 3-5x larger in the non-PWM builds
      (DPRAM slices 36/40/40 vs 118/178; RAMW 18/20/20 vs 59/89)
  * EBR count 54/55/55 vs 49/56 -- no clear pattern
  * DSP count varies 87..97 MULT18X18D, 27..33 ALU54B -- no clear pattern
  * global-net INDEX assignments differ per build (net 0 = CLKOP in 10.81
    and 13.39, net 2/3 = CLKOP in 16.53).  Always re-derive the CMUX map
    per build; do not carry an index across builds.
