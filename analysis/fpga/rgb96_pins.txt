E120 LFE5U-25F : the 96 RGB DATA pins  [confidence HIGH]

HOW FOUND: IOLOGIC*.MODE == IREG_OREG appears 96x in the two non-PWM builds
(Normal 13.39 and LS0allDA 6.69) and only 10x in the three PWM builds
(16.53 / 10.81 / 9.53).  The 96 sites are byte-identical between 13.39 and 6.69.
Mapping those 96 IOLOGIC sites to package pins (accounting for the fact that a
left/right PIC group spans prjtrellis tile rows R,R+1,R+2) gives exactly the 96
left+right-edge pads that my earlier pin census classified as plain fabric-driven
outputs in 16.53 (47 LEFT OUT + 48 RIGHT OUT + 1 LEFT BIDIR).  Zero discrepancy
in either direction.

Vendor spec cross-check: the E120 drives "32 groups of serial RGB data" = 32*3 = 96.
So this pin field is the serial-RGB data bus, 48 pads per side.

The 13 left/right output pads NOT in the set are accounted for:
  10 = RGMII TX  (L2 M1 M2 P1 R1 | J14 J15 K14 K15 K16)
   3 = B2, C3 (left, BIDIR) and D14 (right, BIDIR)  -- role NOT RESOLVED

CORRECTION to my earlier report: I previously called T4 (R44C0B) "MDIO" because it
is a registered BIDIR with IREG_OREG.  T4 is in the 96-set, i.e. it is an RGB data
line, not MDIO.  There is NO MDC/MDIO group: all 10 of the 16.53 IREG_OREG pins
(M3 M4 N1 N3 N4 N5 P3 P4 R5 T4) are members of the 96.  Retract the MDIO claim.
